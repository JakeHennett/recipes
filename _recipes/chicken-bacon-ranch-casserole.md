ingredients:
  - "½ lb bacon"
  - "1 lb chicken breast"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 packet dry ranch mix"
  - "8 oz pasta (shells)"
  - "1 cup mozz"
  - "½ cup cheddar"
  - "Optional spicy cheese"
  - "16 oz alfredo sauce"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 375."
  - "Grease a 9x9 baking dish."
  - "Cook bacon on a cookie sheet for 15 minutes. Flip, drain grease into a skillet, and bake for another 15 minutes."
  - "Heat skillet with bacon grease to medium."
  - "Boil water in 3 qt pot."
  - "While bacon is cooking, dice chicken."
  - "Add oil, ranch mix, and diced chicken to a 1 gallon ziploc bag."
  - "Shake until chicken is coated."
  - "Add chicken to heated skillet and cook until no longer pink."
  - "Add pasta to boiling water and cook until al dente."
  - "Drain pasta and add to the baking dish."
  - "Add cooked chicken and alfredo sauce to baking dish."
  - "Top with all cheese, bacon, salt, and pepper."
  - "Bake until bubbling, about 15 minutes."
  - "Alt. Crock pot chicken and shred. Add chicken and ranch seasoning directly with the pasta"
