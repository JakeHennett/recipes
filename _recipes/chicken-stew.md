ingredients:
  - "2 pouches (~3 lbs) boneless chicken breast"
  - "5 medium/large potatoes"
  - "2 cans whole kernel corn"
  - "4 tbsp butter"
  - "2 large can evaporated milk"
steps:
  - "Fill the bottom of a dutch oven or large pot with ~2 inches of water. Cover and heat on medium high."
  - "Trim chicken breast and add to the pot whole."
  - "Peel (if preferred) and dice potatoes. Do not soak, add directly to the pot."
  - "Drain corn and add to the pot."
  - "When chicken is firm, remove and dice or shred. Return to pot."
  - "Add butter."
  - "Season liberally with salt, pepper, garlic, onion, and paprika."
  - "Add more water if necessary to cover all ingredients."
  - "Cook until potatoes are soft. Taste for seasoning."
  - "Reduce heat to medium low, add evaporated milk, simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Serve with saltine crackers and hot sauce."
