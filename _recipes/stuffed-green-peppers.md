description: ""
tags: []
servings: "6"
time:
  prep: "20 minutes"
  cook: "3-8 hours"
ingredients:
  - "6 large green peppers"
  - "1 lb extra-lean ground beef, browned"
  - "2 tbsp minced onion"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "1/8 tsp garlic powder"
  - "2 cups cooked rice"
  - "15-oz can low-sodium tomato sauce"
  - "3/4 cup shredded mozzerella cheese"
steps:
  - "Cut peppers in half and remove seeds."
  - "Combine all ingredients except peppers and cheese."
  - "Stuff peppers with ground beef mixture. Place in slow cooker."
  - "Cover. Cook on low 6-8 hours, or on high 3-4 hours. Sprinkle with cheese during last 30 minutes."
source: "Hope Comerford, Fix-It and Forget-It Slow Cooker Comfort Foods, p. 213"
