ingredients:
  - "1 lb bacon"
  - "1 lb ground beef"
  - "Onion powder"
  - "1 tsp Garlic salt"
  - "4 oz cream cheese"
  - "1 tbsp ketchup"
  - "1 tbsp mustard"
  - "1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce"
  - "4 large eggs"
  - "¼ cup heavy cream or milk"
  - "1 tsp ground pepper"
  - "1 tsp hot sauce"
  - "8 oz shredded cheddar"
  - "1 tsp dill"
steps:
  - "Cook bacon via any means. Bake for ~30 minutes at 375, flipping every 10 minutes."
  - "Chop bacon into small bits. Alternatively, use bacon bits."
  - "Brown beef in a skillet and drain grease."
  - "Add onion powder and garlic salt to beef."
  - "Add cream cheese, ketchup, mustard, Worcestershire sauce to skillet and cook until combined."
  - "Spread beef mixture into 8x8 baking dish. Top with bacon crumbles."
  - "Crack eggs into bowl, whisk together with heavy cream until combined. Stir in pepper and hot sauce."
  - "Pour egg mixture over beef and bacon."
  - "Top with cheddar cheese."
  - "Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes, or until set and golden on top."
  - "Sprinkle with dill."
