ingredients:
  - "19 oz ground Italian sausage"
  - "23.9 oz spaghetti sauce"
  - "1 box 32 oz chicken broth"
  - "1 box 32 oz beef broth"
  - "16 oz box lasagna noodles"
  - "1 can 14.5 oz diced Italian tomatoes"
  - "15 oz ricotta cheese"
  - "8 oz Italian style cheese blend"
  - "1 cup heavy cream"
  - "Seasonings: salt, pepper, garlic powder, oregano, basil"
steps:
  - "Add sausage to a 6qt Dutch oven and brown."
  - "Add spaghetti sauce and both broths."
  - "Crush noodles and add."
  - "Add diced tomatoes and season liberally."
  - "Bring to a boil."
  - "Submerge noodles, cover, simmer 8 minutes."
  - "Add majority of ricotta and Italian cheeses, and heavy cream."
  - "Cook down 2 more minutes."
  - "Serve accented with cheeses."
